How it works.

Three steps between you and a board full of ideas.

1

Write it down

Type your note, pick a color, and hit Add note — or press Ctrl+Enter to add it fast.

2

Edit in place

Click any note to edit its text, or switch its color from the dots. Every change saves instantly to your browser.

3

Find it fast

Type in the search box to filter the board in real time. Delete a note with the ✕ when you're done with it.
